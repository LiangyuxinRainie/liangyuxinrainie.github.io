About

Hi! I am Yuxin Liang (Chinese: 梁雨欣), an M.S. student in Information and Communication Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Peng Yang.

My research centers on mobile edge computing and edge intelligence, with a particular interest in making generative AI services faster and more resource-efficient. I work on distributed inference, networked resource orchestration, model acceleration, and end-edge collaboration.

Before joining HUST, I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Wuhan University of Technology in June 2024, where I was recognized as an Outstanding Graduate.
